Market intel

Rate benchmarks

Portland-metro asking rates run roughly $2,500–$7,500 per 4-week flight for static bulletins and $3,500–$9,000 for digital (AdQuick, 2026). Statewide, small posters start near $350/4wk and premium digital tops $5,300 (OAG survey). Use these to back into income value on any face you underwrite — same cap-rate math as your STNL work.

Portland is double-locked

Beyond the state moratorium, Portland's own sign code effectively prohibits new billboard construction — new high-impact inventory only comes from converting existing static faces to digital. A conforming static face in the metro that can be converted is the single most valuable thing on your target list.

Non-conforming = cash-flow only

57 of your 170 target signs are non-conforming. They generally can't be rebuilt, upgraded, or relocated, and the permit can die if the structure is destroyed. Price them as declining annuities, not development sites — and use that in negotiation.

Get inside the room

The Oregon Outdoor Advertising Association (OOAA) is the industry trade group working with ODOT on regulation. Membership puts you in the room with every operator on this page — deal flow, rule changes, and retiring owners all surface there first. Also: only ~54 ORS 377.730 licenses exist. File yours now.
